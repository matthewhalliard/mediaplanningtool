# MediaPlanHQ vs Halliard: Which Media Planning Tool Is Right for Your Agency?

If you're evaluating a **MediaPlanHQ alternative**, you're probably a smaller agency or in-house team looking for something accessible — not an enterprise system with a six-figure contract and a three-month implementation. Both MediaPlanHQ and [Halliard](/halliard) sit comfortably in the challenger category: transparent pricing, no enterprise-only gating, and built with independent teams in mind.

But that's roughly where the overlap ends. MediaPlanHQ is purpose-built for operational media workflows — plan creation, invoice reconciliation, and client-facing project management. Halliard is a broader platform: planning, cross-channel measurement, real-time data integrations, and an AI layer that's genuinely doing things other tools aren't. They're answering different questions for their buyers.

This page breaks down both tools honestly — where each wins, where each falls short, and which one fits your workflow.

---

## Quick Overview

**[MediaPlanHQ](/mediaplanhq)** is a SaaS media planning tool that launched with a focus on making plan creation and financial reconciliation less painful for smaller shops. It includes a client request portal, creative project tracking, and straightforward budget management. It's practical, clean, and reasonably priced. What it isn't is an analytics or intelligence platform — there are no AI features, no real-time data connections to ad platforms, and no cross-channel measurement capabilities.

**[Halliard](/halliard)** was built by a media practitioner — Matthew Jacobs brings 15+ years from Starcom, Dentsu, Amazon DSP, and Paramount — with the explicit goal of giving independent agencies tools that have historically been available only to holding company shops. That includes a proprietary cross-channel reach and frequency engine, an AI assistant baked into the planning workflow, agentic buying capabilities (Halliard executed the first fully autonomous media transaction via MCP with PubMatic in December 2025), and real-time integrations with 18+ platforms.

## Pricing Breakdown

MediaPlanHQ's pricing is straightforward. The standard plan runs $99/month flat. The per-seat model is $45/user/month with a minimum of three users — so the effective floor is $135/month. Annual billing drops that to $39/user/month. Observers (read-only) are free, and Collaborators (limited access) run $5/month. For a five-person team on the annual plan, you're looking at roughly $195/month. That's a reasonable entry point for a real tool.

Halliard offers a free plan for individual planners — no credit card, no time limit — which is genuinely useful for solo practitioners or freelancers building out their workflow before committing. Premium and Enterprise plans exist for teams, with pricing available on request. The free tier alone makes the evaluation risk-free in a way MediaPlanHQ doesn't match.

Neither tool requires a multi-year enterprise agreement or a custom procurement process. That distinguishes both of them from most of the legacy competition.

## Where MediaPlanHQ Wins

### Invoice Reconciliation
This is MediaPlanHQ's clearest differentiator. Invoice reconciliation — matching what you planned to buy against what vendors actually invoiced — is genuinely painful in most agency workflows, and MediaPlanHQ has built dedicated functionality around it. If your team spends significant time chasing down billing discrepancies, this is a real productivity unlock.

Halliard's plan-to-proof approach connects planning through measurement, but it doesn't offer the same dedicated reconciliation module. If financial operations is your primary pain point, that gap matters.

### Client Request Portal
MediaPlanHQ includes a client-facing portal where clients can submit requests and track project status. It's the kind of feature that reduces the back-and-forth that clutters small agency operations. For agencies running multiple client relationships with varying levels of client involvement, this is a meaningful workflow improvement.

### Creative Project Management
Alongside media planning, MediaPlanHQ tracks creative deliverables — timelines, statuses, assignments. This is more of a project management layer than a media intelligence feature, but for agencies where media planning and creative production happen under the same roof, having it in one tool has obvious appeal.

### Simplicity
MediaPlanHQ is designed to be easy to adopt. It doesn't require training on complex measurement methodologies or data integrations. If your team just needs to create plans, manage client communication, and reconcile invoices, you don't need — and may not want — a platform with more surface area than you'll use.

## Where Halliard Wins

### Cross-Channel Reach and Frequency
Halliard's proprietary R+F engine runs on a 10,000-person panel covering 210 DMAs. Cross-channel R+F — properly accounting for duplication across linear TV, streaming, social, audio, and digital — has been an enterprise-only capability for years, requiring either a holding company infrastructure or expensive third-party measurement contracts. Halliard brings this to independent agencies at a price point that actually fits their reality.

MediaPlanHQ has no equivalent capability. If cross-channel measurement informs your planning or your client conversations, this comparison isn't particularly close.

### AI Features
Halliard's AI assistant is built into the planning workflow — not bolted on. You can interact with it conversationally to explore scenarios, interrogate data, and accelerate plan development. The What-If Scenario modeling and Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) capabilities add a layer of analytical horsepower that simply doesn't exist in MediaPlanHQ.

MediaPlanHQ has no AI features as of early 2026. That's not a criticism of what it sets out to do — but it's a meaningful gap if you're trying to use AI to move faster or surface insights.

### Agentic Buying
In December 2025, Halliard executed the first fully autonomous media buy via MCP integration with PubMatic. This isn't a hypothetical roadmap item — it happened. The platform can receive a brief, build a plan, and execute a buy without manual intervention. It's early, but it's the most significant forward motion on AI-native buying that exists in the independent agency market.

### Real-Time Platform Integrations
Halliard connects to Google, Meta, TikTok, Spotify, Netflix, YouTube, Twitch, Hulu, and 18+ channels in real time. Your plans can be informed by actual performance data, not just estimates. For agencies who report on campaign performance alongside planning, this closes a loop that otherwise requires exporting data from a dozen platforms and stitching it together manually.

MediaPlanHQ has no real-time platform integrations. It operates as a standalone planning and operations tool.

### Free Plan
The free individual plan is worth noting again, especially for anyone evaluating a **MediaPlanHQ alternative** on a tight timeline. You can sign up at [halliardmedia.com/trytoday](https://halliardmedia.com/trytoday), build a real plan with real features, and make an informed decision — no sales call required.

## Who Should Choose MediaPlanHQ

MediaPlanHQ is a strong fit if:

- **Invoice reconciliation is a top priority.** It's built specifically for this, and it shows.
- **You need a client-facing portal** for request management and project visibility.
- **Your team manages creative production** alongside media and wants one system of record.
- **You want simplicity** over feature depth — a tool that does a few things well without requiring a learning curve.
- **You run a small agency** where the flat standard pricing ($99/mo) makes more sense than per-seat models.

MediaPlanHQ is a solid operational tool. If your primary media planning pain points are administrative and financial — not analytical — it's worth a serious look at [/mediaplanhq](/mediaplanhq).

## Who Should Choose Halliard

Halliard is the better choice if:

- **You want cross-channel R+F** built into your planning — not purchased separately or approximated.
- **AI is part of your workflow**, either for planning acceleration or client-facing insights.
- **You need real-time data** from the platforms you're actually running media on.
- **You're thinking about where media buying is going** — agentic execution, programmatic planning, AI-assisted strategy — and want to be building on a platform that's ahead of that curve.
- **You're an individual planner or small team** who wants to start free and scale up.
- **You manage meaningful media budgets** ($10M–$500M+) and need the analytical infrastructure to match.

[Halliard](https://halliardmedia.com/trytoday) is the right fit for agencies that view media planning as a strategic and analytical function — not just a workflow to be managed.

## The Honest Summary

This is an uncommon comparison because both tools are genuinely accessible — neither requires an enterprise contract, and both were designed with smaller agencies in mind. The question isn't really which one is more affordable. It's which problem you're trying to solve.

If MediaPlanHQ's strengths — reconciliation, client portal, creative management — map to your core pain points, it's a focused tool that does what it promises. There's real value in that.

If you need analytics, AI, real-time integrations, or cross-channel measurement, you're asking MediaPlanHQ to do things it wasn't designed for. Halliard was. And Halliard's free plan means there's no reason not to find out for yourself.

Neither is on G2 or Capterra yet — both are still building review presence alongside product presence. That puts more weight on doing your own evaluation, which both tools make reasonably easy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a free version of MediaPlanHQ?**
No. MediaPlanHQ offers a paid standard plan starting at $99/month, with per-seat pricing at $45/user/month (minimum three users). Observers with read-only access are free, but there's no full free tier. Halliard, by contrast, offers a free plan for individual planners with no credit card required.

**Does MediaPlanHQ have AI features?**
Not as of early 2026. MediaPlanHQ focuses on operational workflows — plan creation, invoice reconciliation, creative project management — and does not currently offer AI-assisted planning, chat interfaces, or machine learning capabilities. If AI is a priority, Halliard is the better fit.

**Can MediaPlanHQ connect to Google, Meta, or TikTok in real time?**
No. MediaPlanHQ does not offer real-time integrations with ad platforms. It operates as a standalone planning and operations system. Halliard connects to 18+ platforms — including Google, Meta, TikTok, Spotify, Netflix, YouTube, Twitch, and Hulu — in real time.

**What does "agentic buying" mean in Halliard's context?**
In December 2025, Halliard executed the first fully autonomous media transaction using an MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration with PubMatic. This means an AI agent — not a human — received a brief, built a plan, and executed the buy. It's an early but real demonstration of what AI-native media buying looks like in practice.

**How does Halliard's R+F engine compare to using a third-party measurement tool?**
Halliard's cross-channel reach and frequency engine uses a proprietary 10,000-person panel covering 210 DMAs. For independent agencies, this capability has historically required either a holding company infrastructure or expensive standalone measurement contracts. Halliard integrates it directly into the planning workflow — not as an add-on, but as a native feature.

**Which tool is better for a small agency just getting started?**
If your immediate needs are operational — plans, reconciliation, client communication — MediaPlanHQ is simpler to adopt. If you want to build on a platform with more analytical depth and AI capability, Halliard's free individual plan is the lowest-risk starting point. You can use it with no commitment and make the call based on your actual workflow.
