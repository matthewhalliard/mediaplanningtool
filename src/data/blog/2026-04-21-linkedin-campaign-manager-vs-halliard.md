# LinkedIn Campaign Manager vs Halliard: Which Media Planning Tool Is Right for You? (2026)

LinkedIn Campaign Manager and Halliard are not competing for the same job. LinkedIn is where you go to run ads on LinkedIn. Halliard is where you go to plan the campaign that includes LinkedIn — alongside TV, CTV, programmatic, paid search, audio, and however many other channels your media mix requires.

But the comparison is worth making, because B2B media planners and marketers increasingly ask: should I evaluate LinkedIn Campaign Manager as my primary planning and activation tool, or do I need something that sits above it? The answer depends on how much of your media budget lives outside LinkedIn — and for most enterprise B2B advertisers, that's a significant share.

---

## What Is LinkedIn Campaign Manager?

[LinkedIn Campaign Manager](/linkedin-campaign-manager) is LinkedIn's self-serve advertising platform for reaching the world's largest professional network — over 1 billion members across 200+ countries. The platform's core advantage is its targeting specificity for B2B audiences: job title, function, seniority, company, industry, company size, professional skills, and educational background.

Recent platform updates have added meaningful capabilities. Media Planner provides reach and frequency forecasting within LinkedIn. Accelerate is an AI-assisted campaign creation workflow that generates budget, audience, ads, and placement recommendations for review before launch. Dynamic UTMs and AI-generated ad drafts have reduced the manual overhead of campaign setup. Campaign Manager also supports account-based marketing (ABM) through Matched Audiences — uploading company lists or contact lists to target specific organizations directly.

Ad formats span Sponsored Content (single image, video, carousel, document), Sponsored Messaging (message ads and conversation ads), Dynamic Ads, and Text Ads. Minimum daily budget starts at $10, making LinkedIn accessible for small B2B campaigns alongside enterprise-scale operations.

## What Is Halliard?

[Halliard](/halliard) is a media planning and buying platform built by Matthew Jacobs — 15+ years across Starcom, Dentsu (SVP Product), Amazon DSP (Senior PM), and Paramount (Senior Director). It's designed for independent agencies and in-house teams managing $10M to $500M+ in annual spend across multiple channels simultaneously.

Where LinkedIn Campaign Manager is a channel-specific buying tool, Halliard is the cross-channel planning layer that sits above all channels — including LinkedIn. The platform provides visual flowchart planning, budget allocation across channels, cross-channel reach and frequency analysis via a proprietary 10,000-person panel, and real-time spend tracking from planned to delivered. Native integrations connect Halliard to Google, Meta, TikTok, Spotify, Netflix, YouTube, Twitch, Hulu, and more — LinkedIn data can be pulled into the planning workspace alongside all other channels.

In December 2025, Halliard executed the first fully autonomous media buy via Model Context Protocol (MCP) with PubMatic. The platform starts free with no credit card required.

## Where LinkedIn Campaign Manager Wins

**Unmatched B2B targeting precision.** No other advertising platform has LinkedIn's depth of first-party professional data. Job title, function, seniority level, company, industry, company size, and skills targeting is deterministic — built from users' own professional profiles, not inferred from browsing behavior. For B2B advertisers trying to reach enterprise decision-makers, procurement directors, or C-suite executives by title and company, LinkedIn is the standard.

**Account-based marketing at scale.** Matched Audiences lets B2B marketers upload CRM contact lists or target account lists and serve ads directly to named companies or individuals — a capability that's central to enterprise ABM strategy. The integration between LinkedIn Campaign Manager and major CRMs and MAP platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo) makes it a natural extension of the B2B marketing stack.

**AI-assisted campaign creation.** The Accelerate feature reduces the friction of campaign setup significantly. For teams running many campaigns, the AI-generated draft — budget, audience, ad copy, placements — creates a starting point that doesn't require hours of manual configuration before review.

**Native to the channel.** LinkedIn Campaign Manager is the only way to run LinkedIn ads. There's no alternative — you have to use it. Any comparison is really about what sits around it, not what replaces it.

**Performance forecasting within LinkedIn.** The Media Planner feature provides estimated reach, frequency, and impression projections for LinkedIn-specific audiences before campaign launch. For teams optimizing within LinkedIn's ecosystem, this forecasting is useful.

## Where Halliard Wins

**Multi-channel visibility in one workspace.** LinkedIn Campaign Manager shows you what's happening on LinkedIn. Halliard shows you what's happening on LinkedIn, and also on Meta, Google, TikTok, Spotify, CTV, linear TV, programmatic, and audio — simultaneously, in a single dashboard. For B2B advertisers running multi-channel campaigns (which is most enterprise B2B marketing), this visibility is essential.

**Cross-channel reach and frequency analysis.** LinkedIn's Media Planner tells you how many LinkedIn members your campaign will reach. Halliard's proprietary 10,000-person panel tells you how many unique people your combined LinkedIn + programmatic + YouTube + audio plan will reach, at what frequency, across the whole campaign. These are different insights serving different planning decisions.

**Campaign planning that precedes any channel.** Halliard starts before the channel selection — at the strategic layer where budget allocation, channel mix decisions, and reach modeling happen. LinkedIn Campaign Manager starts at the point of execution within LinkedIn. For teams that need to justify a $5M media plan to a CMO before launching any campaigns, Halliard's planning workflow is where that work lives.

**Approval workflows and documentation.** The structured approval workflow in Halliard — with version history, client feedback capture, and budget authorization trails — solves a collaboration problem that Campaign Manager doesn't address. Getting a multi-channel plan approved by legal, finance, and the client before execution requires more infrastructure than a self-serve ad platform provides.

**Institutional memory.** Halliard retains every plan, result, and performance benchmark inside a single workspace. When a media director joins a B2B marketing team and inherits previous campaigns, the institutional knowledge about what LinkedIn targeting segments performed, at what frequency, against what offers, is preserved in Halliard — not scattered across Campaign Manager exports.

**Halliard** offers a free plan for individual planners, with premium tiers for teams and agencies.

## The B2B Media Plan Reality

Most enterprise B2B advertising is not 100% LinkedIn. The typical mix for a $2M annual B2B media budget might include LinkedIn (40%), Google Search (20%), programmatic display and video (20%), YouTube (10%), and audio/podcast (10%). The LinkedIn allocation is large and important — but the strategic question of how to allocate across all five requires a different tool than Campaign Manager.

Halliard is designed to answer the "how should we allocate the budget?" question before Campaign Manager answers the "how should we configure the LinkedIn campaigns?" question. Both tools are part of the same workflow — they just operate at different stages.

## FAQ

**Does LinkedIn Campaign Manager charge a platform fee?**
No. LinkedIn Campaign Manager charges only for media spend — CPC or CPM based on auction pricing. There are no platform fees. The $10/day minimum applies at the campaign level. Halliard's free plan also has no platform fee for individual planners.

**What is LinkedIn's Accelerate feature?**
Accelerate is LinkedIn's AI-assisted campaign creation tool. It uses AI to generate a complete campaign draft — including budget recommendations, audience targeting, ad copy, and placements — for the advertiser to review and modify before launch. It reduces the manual configuration burden of setting up LinkedIn campaigns.

**Can Halliard pull LinkedIn campaign data?**
Yes. Halliard integrates with channel platforms including LinkedIn to pull performance data into the planning workspace for real-time spend tracking and historical benchmarking.

**What is LinkedIn's Media Planner?**
Media Planner is a forecasting tool within LinkedIn Campaign Manager that provides estimated reach, frequency, and impression projections for a proposed campaign targeting configuration. It operates within LinkedIn's ecosystem and doesn't model cross-channel reach.
