**Web-based media planning and buying platform with attribution insights and fast invoice reconciliation**

GaleForce Media (by GaleForce Digital) is a browser-based platform built for agencies managing traditional and digital media campaigns. It handles the full planning-to-reconciliation workflow in a single tool—covering TV, radio, digital, and print—with a focus on reducing the manual effort that slows down media teams. The platform's signature features include spot-level attribution connecting ad airings to web traffic, and invoice reconciliation that reportedly takes a 40-hour task down to about 3 hours.

## Key Features

### Unified Media Planning & Buying
- Planning and buying across broadcast TV, radio, digital, print, and OOH in one system
- Nielsen TV/Radio, Comscore TV, and Eastlan Radio ratings integration included
- Combined reach and frequency calculations across TV and radio
- Multi-market and multi-worksheet capabilities
- Electronic ordering and vendor management

### Attribution Insights
- Spot-level attribution connecting individual TV and radio ad airings to web traffic
- Google Analytics integration for campaign effectiveness measurement
- Real-time impact analysis at the spot and campaign level
- Data-driven optimization based on actual traffic response
- Patent-pending technology unique to GaleForce Media

### Invoice Reconciliation
- Automated invoice matching against planned buys
- Reconciliation that reduces 40-hour manual processes to approximately 3 hours
- Discrepancy flagging and resolution workflow
- Streamlined billing preparation for client invoicing

### AI Planning Assistant (GaiL)
- AI-powered predictive recommendations for media planning
- Launched November 2025; patent-pending
- Automates routine planning suggestions based on campaign history
- Designed to help planners move faster without sacrificing accuracy

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is GaleForce Media free?**
GaleForce Media is not free, but it offers a two-week free trial with no setup fees. Paid plans are annual subscriptions priced based on agency size and features.

**How much does GaleForce Media cost?**
Pricing is not publicly listed. GaleForce Digital offers customized annual subscriptions. Contact them directly for a quote. The platform is positioned as the most affordable option in its category.

**What ratings data is included?**
Nielsen TV/Radio, Comscore TV, and Eastlan Radio ratings are included in the platform. This eliminates the need to manage separate ratings subscriptions for most traditional media planning workflows.

**How does GaleForce Media compare to Strata?**
[Strata](/strata) is a long-established broadcast buying platform with deep traffic and posting capabilities. GaleForce Media offers a more modern interface, includes attribution insights, and is often cited as more affordable. Users who switched from Strata frequently mention the reconciliation speed and support responsiveness as differentiators.

**What is Attribution Insights?**
Attribution Insights is GaleForce Media's proprietary feature that links individual TV and radio spot airings to corresponding web traffic spikes. It integrates with Google Analytics to show which spots are driving measurable site activity, giving media buyers direct ROI evidence for traditional media.

**What is GaiL?**
GaiL is GaleForce Media's AI-powered planning assistant, launched in November 2025. It provides predictive recommendations within the platform to help planners work more efficiently. The technology is patent-pending.

**Does GaleForce Media handle digital buying as well as traditional?**
Yes. The platform supports digital media planning and buying alongside traditional channels (TV, radio, print, OOH). This makes it practical for agencies running integrated campaigns without needing a separate digital-only tool.

## GaleForce Media vs Alternatives

### GaleForce Media vs Bionic
[Bionic](/bionic) is a modern media planning platform focused on plan building, collaboration, and flowcharts. GaleForce Media covers more of the buying and reconciliation workflow, including spot attribution and invoice matching. Agencies that need end-to-end traditional media workflow management tend to favor GaleForce; those prioritizing plan presentation and collaboration lean toward Bionic.

### GaleForce Media vs Strata
[Strata](/strata) (now FreeWheel) is the long-standing broadcast buying standard. GaleForce Media is a newer alternative that users often describe as more modern, more affordable, and better-supported. Strata has deeper traffic and posting capabilities for broadcast-heavy shops; GaleForce Media offers broader digital integration and attribution features.

### GaleForce Media vs Bluhorn
[Bluhorn](/bluhorn) is another independent media planning and buying platform for traditional media. GaleForce Media differentiates with its Attribution Insights feature and GaiL AI assistant. Both serve similar agency types and are often compared on price and support quality.

### GaleForce Media vs Advantage
[Advantage](/advantage) is a full agency ERP covering project management, accounting, and media operations. GaleForce Media focuses specifically on media planning, buying, and reconciliation. Agencies that need finance and project management alongside media may prefer Advantage; those focused purely on media workflow often find GaleForce Media more streamlined.
