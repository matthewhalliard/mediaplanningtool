**Sports audience measurement and market analytics that connect fan passion to business results**

Fifty5Blue Sports Market Analytics & Research is the dedicated sports vertical within Fifty5Blue's (formerly Kantar Media) measurement portfolio. It measures how fan engagement translates to real business outcomes — covering sports viewership, sponsorship valuation, and audience analytics across broadcast and streaming platforms. For leagues, rights holders, broadcasters, agencies, and sponsors, it provides the data needed to value, plan, and optimize sports media investments. Part of Fifty5Blue following the $1 billion spin-off from Kantar Group (H.I.G. Capital acquisition, August 2025).

## Key Features

### 📺 **Sports Viewership Measurement**
- Audience measurement across linear TV, streaming, and digital platforms for sports events
- Real-time ratings and viewership data during live events
- Cross-platform measurement capturing how fans watch across screens
- Event-level and league-level audience reporting

### 🏟️ **Fan Engagement Analytics**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Fifty5Blue Sports Market Analytics?**
It's the sports-focused measurement vertical within Fifty5Blue that measures sports viewership, fan engagement, and the business impact of sports media investments across platforms.

**How does it differ from general audience measurement?**
Sports analytics is tailored for the unique dynamics of live sports — real-time measurement, event-level reporting, sponsorship valuation, and fan engagement metrics that don't apply to general entertainment.

**Which sports and markets does it cover?**
Coverage varies by market. Fifty5Blue operates in 80+ countries, with sports measurement particularly strong in markets where they hold TV measurement contracts (Latin America via Ibope, European markets, etc.).

**Can it measure streaming sports viewership?**
Yes. Fifty5Blue's hybrid measurement approach combines panel data with digital data to capture sports viewing across linear TV, streaming, and digital platforms.

## Sports Analytics vs Alternatives

### vs Nielsen Sports
Nielsen provides sports ratings in the US market. Fifty5Blue Sports Analytics has broader international coverage, particularly in Latin America (via Ibope) and European markets. US agencies typically use Nielsen; international campaigns often rely on Fifty5Blue.

### vs Comscore
[Comscore](/comscore) provides cross-platform measurement including sports content. Fifty5Blue Sports Analytics is more specialized — dedicated sports measurement, sponsorship valuation, and fan engagement analytics beyond audience counting.

### vs GWI Sports Data
[GWI](/gwi) offers survey-based sports fan profiling and attitudes. Fifty5Blue Sports Analytics provides actual viewership measurement and real-time ratings. GWI tells you about sports fans; Fifty5Blue measures what they actually watch.
