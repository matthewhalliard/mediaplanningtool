**Agentic AI media planning for intent-driven programmatic audiences — from brief to activation.**

NanoQ is an agentic AI media planner from Nano Interactive designed to speed up programmatic audience planning, creation, and activation.
From a natural-language brief, NanoQ can generate bespoke audience segments and recommend relevant off-the-shelf segments from Nano’s “Intent Library,” aiming to replace fragmented, manual planning workflows.

## Key Features

### Brief → Segment Generation
- Turn a natural-language brief into custom audience segments
- Surface recommended “Intent Topics” (prebuilt segments) from a continuously refreshed taxonomy
- Reduce planning cycles from hours to minutes (per Nano)

### Privacy-Friendly Intent Targeting
- Built on Nano Interactive’s intent data intelligence (no reliance on third-party cookies)
- Designed to maintain transparency and advertiser control versus black-box optimization

### Multi-Market Planning at Scale
- Activate audiences across many regions/languages without recreating segments market-by-market
- Intended to support global teams with consistent taxonomy + localized adjustments

### Controls, Testing, and Governance
- Topic prioritization and category refinements
- Sentiment thresholds and market-level adjustments
- Multivariate testing of segments to compare performance

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is NanoQ?**  
NanoQ is an agentic AI media planner from Nano Interactive that uses natural-language briefs to generate programmatic audience segments and activation recommendations.

**Who is NanoQ for?**  
NanoQ is positioned for media planners and programmatic teams at agencies and brands who want faster audience planning and scalable activation across multiple markets.

**Does NanoQ replace a DSP?**  
No. NanoQ is a planning and audience creation layer; activation still depends on the buying/activation partners in your stack.

**Is NanoQ privacy-friendly?**  
NanoQ is built on privacy-friendly intent data intelligence (rather than identity-based third-party cookie targeting) and is designed for privacy-constrained environments.

**Can NanoQ support global or multilingual campaigns?**  
Yes — Nano says NanoQ can activate audiences across 100 languages/markets without repeating manual steps market-by-market.

**Does NanoQ share or train on my data?**  
Nano states that NanoQ uses industry-leading LLMs without sharing or training on client data.

**How do I access NanoQ?**  
Nano offers a demo and a waiting list via their AI site.
