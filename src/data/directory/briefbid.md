**The largest open marketplace for discovering and connecting with specialized media vendors across all advertising channels**

BriefBid automates vendor discovery and RFP processes, connecting advertisers with 700+ vetted media partners across digital, OOH, DOOH, mobile, audio, and native channels. Used by agencies including Publicis, The Media Kitchen, and Direct Agents, the platform is free for agencies and brands — vendors pay a commission only on successful placements.

## Pricing

BriefBid operates on a freemium, commission-based model:

- **Free for Agencies and Brands**: No cost to create an account, browse vendors, submit RFPs, or compare proposals
- **Vendor Commission**: Publishers and media vendors pay a commission on deals closed through the platform — this cost is not passed to buyers
- **No Hidden Fees**: Buyers face no additional charges beyond their media spend

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is BriefBid free?**
Yes — BriefBid is completely free for agencies and brands. There are no setup fees, subscription costs, or platform charges for buyers. Vendors pay a commission on placements made through the platform, but that cost is not passed on to advertisers.

**How much does BriefBid cost for media vendors?**
Vendors pay a commission only on deals successfully closed through the platform. The commission structure is transparent and does not inflate media costs for buyers. Specific rates are discussed during vendor onboarding.

**How does BriefBid compare to Bionic?**
[Bionic](/bionic) is a media planning and flowchart tool focused on campaign management and trafficking workflows, while BriefBid is a vendor discovery and RFP marketplace. Many agencies use both: BriefBid to find and vet vendors, and a planning tool to manage the resulting buys. See our [Bionic listing](/bionic) for a full comparison.

**How does BriefBid compare to Mediaocean?**
[Mediaocean](/mediaocean) is a comprehensive media management platform covering buying, billing, and reconciliation. BriefBid focuses specifically on the vendor discovery and RFP stage. For agencies that already have a buying system in place, BriefBid adds a front-end procurement layer without replacing existing infrastructure.

**How many vendors are in the BriefBid network?**
BriefBid maintains relationships with 700+ vetted media vendors spanning digital display, video, OOH, DOOH, audio, mobile, and native channels, including niche and vertical-specific publishers.

**Does BriefBid support international campaigns?**
Yes. BriefBid provides international vendor coverage, making it suitable for global and multi-market campaign planning.

**What channels does BriefBid support?**
BriefBid covers DOOH, OOH, mobile, digital display, video, audio, and native advertising across both domestic and international markets.

## BriefBid vs Alternatives

### BriefBid vs Bionic
[Bionic](/bionic) is a media planning platform with flowchart, RFP, and trafficking features built for campaign management workflows. BriefBid is specifically a vendor discovery marketplace. Bionic requires a subscription; BriefBid is free for buyers. Teams that need vendor discovery alongside full campaign management often use BriefBid for the procurement stage and a dedicated planning tool for execution.

### BriefBid vs Mediaocean
[Mediaocean](/mediaocean) is an enterprise media management system covering planning, buying, billing, and reconciliation. BriefBid sits upstream in the workflow, helping planners identify and evaluate vendors before a buy is made. The two tools are largely complementary rather than competitive.

### BriefBid vs Mediatool
[Mediatool](/mediatool) offers media planning, campaign tracking, and reporting functionality in a subscription platform. BriefBid focuses exclusively on the RFP and vendor discovery process. Teams that already have a planning tool may add BriefBid for vendor sourcing without replacing existing workflows.

### BriefBid vs The Trade Desk
[The Trade Desk](/the-trade-desk) is a programmatic DSP for automated media buying across open-internet inventory. BriefBid is a marketplace for direct-response RFPs covering both digital and traditional (OOH, DOOH, audio) channels. They serve different procurement models: automated bidding vs. direct vendor proposals.

### BriefBid vs Quantcast
Quantcast is an audience intelligence and programmatic platform. BriefBid is a vendor discovery and RFP tool. There is limited overlap — Quantcast is best for programmatic activation, while BriefBid handles direct and specialty vendor procurement.
