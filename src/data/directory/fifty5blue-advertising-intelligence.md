**Unified competitive ad spend monitoring across every media channel in one dashboard**

Fifty5Blue Advertising Intelligence (formerly Kantar Media Ad Intelligence) provides a unified view of advertising placement and spend across all major media touchpoints — TV, digital, OOH, radio, print, and cinema. It's the competitive monitoring layer that agencies, brands, and media owners use to track where competitors are investing, verify flight execution, and identify market trends. Following Fifty5Blue's $1 billion spin-off from Kantar Group (acquired by H.I.G. Capital, August 2025), the Advertising Intelligence product continues to serve as one of the most comprehensive ad spend databases globally.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Fifty5Blue Advertising Intelligence?**
It's a competitive advertising monitoring platform that tracks ad placement, spend, and share of voice across TV, digital, OOH, radio, print, and cinema. Formerly Kantar Media Ad Intelligence.

**How much does Fifty5Blue Advertising Intelligence cost?**

## Fifty5Blue Advertising Intelligence vs Alternatives

### vs Nielsen Ad Intel
Nielsen Ad Intel is the dominant US ad spend database. Fifty5Blue Advertising Intelligence has stronger international coverage across 80+ countries. US agencies often use Nielsen domestically and Fifty5Blue globally.

### vs MediaRadar/Vivvix
MediaRadar acquired Vivvix (Kantar's former US ad intelligence unit) in 2023. Fifty5Blue Advertising Intelligence is the global product that remained within Fifty5Blue. MediaRadar is US-focused; Fifty5Blue covers more markets and media channels.

### vs Comscore
[Comscore](/comscore) focuses on audience measurement and Proximic activation. Fifty5Blue Advertising Intelligence focuses on competitive ad spend and placement. Different use cases — measurement vs. competitive monitoring.

### vs SRDS
[SRDS](/srds-media-planning) is a media rate and planning database. Fifty5Blue Advertising Intelligence tracks what competitors actually spent and where they placed ads. SRDS helps you plan; Fifty5Blue Ad Intel helps you benchmark and compete.
