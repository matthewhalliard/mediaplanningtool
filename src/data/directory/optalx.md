**Creative analytics platform measuring both performance and brand equity through proprietary Intermix Modeling™**

optalX launched in mid-2025 as a creative analytics platform built around Intermix Modeling™ — a proprietary AI approach that measures the combined impact of direct response performance and brand equity from creative assets. The platform generates weekly automated presentation-ready reports and provides predictive recommendations across paid social, search, CTV, and other digital channels. Pricing starts at $29/month with a 14-day free trial on all paid tiers.

- Real-time optimization signals based on Intermix Modeling™ outputs
- Media buy recommendations informed by creative performance data

## optalX vs Alternatives

### optalX vs TapClicks
TapClicks is a marketing reporting and analytics platform used broadly across agency workflows. It focuses on aggregating media data from multiple sources into unified dashboards. optalX is specifically built around creative performance scoring and brand equity measurement through Intermix Modeling™ — a more specialized use case than TapClicks' general reporting capabilities.

### optalX vs Funnel
Funnel is a data aggregation and marketing analytics platform designed to consolidate and clean marketing data from dozens of sources. optalX uses connected data to produce AI-driven creative analysis and predictive recommendations. The two serve different functions: Funnel handles data infrastructure, optalX handles creative intelligence.

### optalX vs Pixis Prism
[Pixis Prism](/pixis-prism) is an AI-powered creative intelligence and optimization platform. Like optalX, it focuses on creative performance analysis. Both platforms aim to quantify creative impact, though optalX differentiates through its Intermix Modeling™ approach to measuring brand equity alongside direct response.

### optalX vs Measured
[Measured](/measured) focuses on incrementality testing and marketing mix modeling — measuring whether media channels drive incremental outcomes. optalX focuses on creative-level performance analysis within channels. The two address different measurement questions and could be complementary for teams that want both channel-level and creative-level attribution.

### optalX vs Northbeam
[Northbeam](/northbeam) is a multi-touch attribution platform for performance marketers, particularly in e-commerce. optalX's Intermix Modeling™ goes beyond click-based attribution to capture brand equity. For direct-response advertisers, Northbeam offers more established attribution infrastructure; optalX offers deeper creative scoring.
