## What is GWI?

GWI (formerly Global Web Index) is a consumer research platform used by brands, agencies, and media planners worldwide to understand audiences through survey-backed data — not scraped web content. With 250K+ profiling points drawn from 1.4 million annual consumer interviews across 52+ markets, GWI powers audience targeting, media planning, and competitive intelligence for teams of all sizes.

In 2025–2026, GWI introduced a **free self-service tier**, an AI insights agent called **Agent Spark**, an AI-powered presentation builder called **GWI Canvas**, and **MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration** that embeds GWI data directly inside ChatGPT, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GWI really free?
Yes. GWI's Free plan costs $0/month with no credit card required. You get 10 Agent Spark prompts/month, unlimited GWI Canvas decks, and access to 50K profiling points across global markets. It's a genuine freemium model — not a limited trial.

### How does Agent Spark differ from ChatGPT or other AI chatbots?
Agent Spark is grounded exclusively in GWI's verified survey data from 1.4 million annual consumer interviews — not scraped internet content. Every answer comes from real human responses, making it reliable for strategic decisions. On Pro plan, Agent Spark also works inside ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot via MCP integration.

### What is GWI's MCP integration?
MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants access external data sources. GWI's MCP integration, launched in November 2025 with Claude and expanded to ChatGPT and Copilot in January 2026, lets your AI tools pull verified consumer insights directly from GWI without leaving your chat.

### How does GWI compare to MRI-Simmons?
GWI covers 52+ markets globally with a free entry tier and AI-powered tools (Agent Spark, Canvas). [MRI-Simmons](/mri-simmons) is US-focused with deeper media consumption data and is typically used by large agencies. GWI is more accessible and global; MRI-Simmons offers more granular US media data.

### What data does GWI cover?
GWI surveys nearly 1.4 million consumers annually across 52+ markets. Data spans demographics, psychographics, media consumption, brand attitudes, purchase behaviors, social media usage, technology adoption, and more — totaling 250K+ profiling points on the full platform.

### How often is GWI data updated?
Core data is updated quarterly. Pro plan users can access 15+ years of historical trended data. Agent Spark pulls from the most recent available dataset.

### Can I export GWI data?
Yes. Charts can be exported on Plus and above. Canvas decks can be exported as presentations (watermark-free on Plus and Pro). Pro and Enterprise plans include API access for automated data exports and integration with BI tools.

### How much does GWI cost for a team?
For a single user, GWI Plus is $150/month ($1,500/year). For teams, GWI offers volume discounts on the Teams plan — contact sales for a custom quote. The Free plan works well for individual users who need occasional access.

## GWI vs Alternatives

### GWI vs MRI-Simmons
GWI offers global coverage (52+ markets) with a free tier and AI tools, while [MRI-Simmons](/mri-simmons) provides deeper US-focused media consumption data. Choose GWI for global audience research and self-service AI; choose MRI-Simmons for detailed US media planning and buying integration.

### GWI vs Comscore
[Comscore](/comscore) specializes in cross-platform audience measurement and digital analytics, while GWI focuses on survey-based consumer insights and attitudes. GWI gives you the "why" behind consumer behavior; Comscore gives you the "what" in terms of digital consumption.

### GWI vs Nielsen
[Nielsen](/nielsen-media-impact) is the legacy standard for TV ratings and media measurement. GWI offers broader attitudinal and psychographic data with modern AI tools. Nielsen for media buying currency; GWI for audience understanding and strategic planning.

### GWI vs Scarborough
[Scarborough](/scarborough) provides local market consumer data, ideal for regional media planning. GWI offers global coverage with a more modern platform and AI tools. Choose Scarborough for hyperlocal US insights; GWI for national and global campaigns.

### GWI vs Resonate
[Resonate](/resonate) focuses on real-time audience activation and AI-driven targeting. GWI provides deeper survey-backed research with more profiling points. GWI for strategic planning and research; Resonate for activation-ready audience segments.
