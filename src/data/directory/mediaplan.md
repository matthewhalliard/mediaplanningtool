**MediaPlan is an AI workspace where an agent called Atlas turns a one-line brief into a channel plan with budgets and benchmarks, then edits it by chat and builds the client deck around it.**

MediaPlan is built in Montreal and sells to working media planners, independent agencies and in-house teams. It runs as a hosted cloud workspace, and also offers a Local Mode that works directly on a planner's own Excel files in the browser with their own Anthropic API key. Pricing is published in Canadian dollars: a free tier, a Pro plan at $49 CAD per month, and Local Mode free of charge.

## What is MediaPlan?

MediaPlan positions itself as "the agentic media-planning workspace". Instead of a flowchart grid you fill in by hand, the starting point is a conversation: you describe the campaign (budget, goal, audience, timing) and Atlas, MediaPlan's planning agent, proposes a channel mix with a budget, role and benchmark for each line. You then change the plan by asking, for example "shift $4,000 from Display into Paid Search", and the agent rebalances and saves it.

The product covers the planning workflow end to end in five steps: brief, plan, vendors, deck and export. That makes it closer to an AI planning assistant than to an operations platform. It does not buy media, traffic creative or reconcile invoices, which is where tools like [MediaPlanHQ](/mediaplanhq) or [Bionic](/bionic) sit.

## Key Features

### Plan builder by chat

Describe a campaign and Atlas drafts a full channel plan. Edits happen in the same conversation: move budget, swap tactics, or rebalance the mix, and the plan updates in place. Vendors can be pulled into the plan from your own vendor library as it builds.

### Arithmetic that reconciles

MediaPlan makes a specific point of the numbers adding up. Channel lines are re-totalled against the plan budget in code on every edit, allocated and remaining budget stay on screen, and a mix that runs over the total is flagged. Impressions, clicks, conversions and revenue are calculated from each line's own CPM, CPC, CPA or ROAS benchmark rather than written by the model, so the same plan returns the same projections every time.

### Benchmark checks

Plan lines are checked against curated CPM, CPC and ROAS ranges, with a one-click benchmark check on any line.

### Strategy decks

Point MediaPlan at a finished plan and it writes the presentation around it: a slide outline with talking points and a visual direction, one slide per tactic, and speaker notes on every slide. Decks export to a real .pptx with the notes attached as PowerPoint notes, to PDF, or present full-screen.

### Vendor discovery and RFPs

A curated library of media owners can be filtered by market (US, Canada, UK, Germany, France and others) and channel, or matched to a brief by the agent. MediaPlan also generates RFPs to send to the vendors you pick.

### Client intelligence and audiences

MediaPlan generates audience profiles backed by US Census and Statistics Canada data, plus personas you can chat with to pressure-test creative and messaging. Profiles can be dropped straight into a plan or a brief.

### Excel and PowerPoint export

Excel exports are written as real numeric cells for budgets, projections and dates under a TOTAL row, so planners can sum and pivot them. Plans and decks also export to PowerPoint and PDF.

## Local Mode

Local Mode is MediaPlan's main point of difference. You open a folder on your computer, add your own Anthropic API key, and the same agent reads and edits your actual Excel files in place, keeping every tab and your formatting. It runs in Chrome or Edge using the browser's File System Access API, so there is nothing to install, and MediaPlan says files and the key never leave your machine. You pay Anthropic directly for usage at cost, with no MediaPlan subscription.

This is useful for agencies with confidential client data, or planners whose plans already live in house spreadsheet templates they do not want to rebuild in a new tool.

## Who it's for

- **Individual media planners and freelancers** who want a fast first draft of a plan and a deck from a brief.
- **Independent agencies** producing many plans and pitch decks that need the numbers to reconcile.
- **In-house teams** that keep plans in Excel and want AI help without moving files into a new cloud system (via Local Mode).

It is a weaker fit for enterprise agencies that need buying, trafficking, approvals and finance workflows in the same system, or reach and frequency modelling from a measurement panel.

## MediaPlan vs Alternatives

### MediaPlan vs MediaPlanHQ

[MediaPlanHQ](/mediaplanhq) is a planning and operations platform: flowcharts, budget tracking, invoice reconciliation and client requests, from $39 per user per month. MediaPlan is agent-first and stops at the plan and the deck. Choose MediaPlanHQ for running campaigns' budgets and paperwork; choose MediaPlan for drafting plans and presentations quickly.

### MediaPlan vs Adlib Planner

[Adlib Planner](/adlib-planner) also automates media planning with AI. MediaPlan's distinguishing features are the deck generator with speaker notes and the bring-your-own-key Local Mode that edits existing Excel files.

### MediaPlan vs Halliard

[Halliard](/halliard) also drafts a media plan from a brief, and models reach and frequency for the plan; it is published by the same company as this directory. MediaPlan focuses on budget benchmarks, decks, vendor RFPs and working on local spreadsheets.

### MediaPlan vs general-purpose chatbots

MediaPlan's own pitch is against using a general chatbot for planning: totals and projections are computed in code rather than generated as text, and plans export as working spreadsheets rather than prose.
