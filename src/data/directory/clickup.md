## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is ClickUp free?**
Yes. ClickUp offers a Free Forever plan with unlimited tasks, unlimited users, and essential features. Paid plans start at $7/user/month (billed annually) for the Unlimited tier.

**How much does ClickUp cost for teams?**
ClickUp's Unlimited plan is $7/user/month billed annually. The Business plan is $12/user/month. Enterprise pricing is custom. AI add-ons (ClickUp Brain) are $9/user/month on top of base plan pricing.

**Is ClickUp good for media planning?**
ClickUp is a general-purpose work management tool that can be adapted for media planning workflows using custom fields, templates, and automations. It works well for campaign project management and coordination but lacks the media-specific features — such as reach/frequency analysis, platform integrations, and media buy reconciliation — found in dedicated media planning tools like [Halliard](/halliard) or [Bionic](/bionic).

**How does ClickUp compare to Monday.com?**
Both ClickUp and Monday.com are all-in-one work management platforms. ClickUp generally offers more granular customization, a more feature-rich free tier, and stronger AI capabilities. Monday.com is often preferred for simpler, more visual project tracking. G2 rates ClickUp at 4.7/5 based on over 11,000 reviews.

**How does ClickUp compare to Asana?**
ClickUp and Asana are direct competitors. ClickUp has a stronger free plan, more built-in features (docs, chat, time tracking), and more aggressive AI development. Asana is often seen as cleaner and easier to configure for enterprise workflow governance.

**What AI features does ClickUp offer?**
ClickUp offers ClickUp Brain for writing, summarization, and task creation; Autopilot Agents for workflow automation; Super Agents for proactive insight surfacing; and AI Notetaker for meeting transcription. Advanced AI requires the Brain add-on ($9/user/month) or the Everything AI tier.

**Can ClickUp replace a dedicated media planning tool?**
For basic campaign coordination and project tracking, yes. For media-specific workflows — platform data integrations, reach/frequency modeling, buy approvals, and post-buy analysis — a purpose-built tool like [Halliard](/halliard) or [Basis](/basis) is a better fit.

## ClickUp vs Alternatives

### ClickUp vs Monday.com
Monday.com is ClickUp's closest direct competitor in the work management space. ClickUp offers more built-in features, a more generous free tier, and more advanced AI tools. Monday.com is favored by teams who prioritize visual simplicity and faster initial setup. For media teams, neither replaces a purpose-built media planning tool for complex campaign management.

### ClickUp vs Asana
Asana is the enterprise go-to for structured workflow governance. ClickUp competes with more features per price tier and stronger AI development. Asana's interface is generally seen as cleaner; ClickUp's is more customizable but can require more configuration time.

### ClickUp vs Halliard
[Halliard](/halliard) is purpose-built for media teams — with channel integrations, reach/frequency analysis, buy approvals, and performance benchmarking built in. ClickUp covers the project management and coordination layer well, but media-specific workflows require workarounds. Teams often use ClickUp for general marketing operations and Halliard for media planning specifically.

### ClickUp vs Camphouse
[Camphouse](/camphouse) is a dedicated media planning platform for global marketing teams. ClickUp can be adapted for similar coordination purposes but lacks media-specific data features. For teams whose primary challenge is campaign project management rather than media-specific planning, ClickUp may be sufficient.
