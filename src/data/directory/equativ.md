**The independent end-to-end media platform with agentic AI that turns a brief into an activated programmatic campaign in minutes**

Equativ (formerly Smart AdServer) is a global independent ad tech company powering media buyers and publishers through its flagship platform, Maestro by Equativ. In December 2025, Equativ launched the Media Planning Agent — the first AI co-planner embedded directly into a curation platform — which transforms media briefs into actionable deal strategies, cutting planning time by up to 40%. Trusted by all six Big Six Advertising Agencies and used to create 30,000+ deals across 500K+ publishers and apps, Equativ is one of the few independent DSP/SSP platforms operating at global scale.

## Key Features

### AI Media Planning Agent (Launched December 2025)
- Share a campaign brief (brand, product, budget, objectives, audience, format) and the AI agent instantly creates a personalized activation plan
- Automatically finds top audiences, activates Advanced Meta Deals, and bundles multiple tactics under one ID
- Analyzes 400K+ sites and apps to prioritize premium supply, first-party data, and relevant audience segments
- Cuts planning time by up to 40%, freeing media teams for strategy rather than spreadsheets
- Every recommendation includes a clear explanation — teams stay in full control to review, refine, and approve
- Rated by IPG Mediabrands, Omnicom Media Group, and Kinesso as a genuine step-change in planning workflow

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Equativ Maestro?**
Maestro by Equativ is an all-in-one curation and campaign management platform for media buyers. It connects buyers to premium programmatic inventory across 500K+ publishers, enables first-party data activation, manages deals, and — since December 2025 — includes an AI Media Planning Agent that converts campaign briefs directly into activated deals.

**How does Equativ's AI Media Planning Agent work?**
You share a campaign brief with brand, product, budget, objectives, audience, and format details. The AI agent interprets your KPIs, finds top audiences, packages Advanced Meta Deals, and presents a ready-to-activate plan — all within the Maestro interface. You review and approve before any spend is committed. The agent cuts planning time by up to 40%.

**Is Equativ independent from the major walled gardens?**
Yes. Equativ operates as a fully independent media platform — it is not affiliated with Google, Meta, Amazon, or any walled-garden ecosystem. This gives buyers transparent access to premium open-web and CTV inventory without proprietary data conflicts.

**What is GreenPMPs™?**
GreenPMPs™ is Equativ's sustainability feature within Maestro that removes climate-risk sites with unnecessarily high emissions (as measured by Scope3) from campaign targeting with a single toggle. Equativ has delivered 50B+ GreenPMP impressions to date.

**How does Equativ compare to The Trade Desk?**
[The Trade Desk](/tools/the-trade-desk) is a pure-play DSP operating on the buy side only. Equativ bridges both supply and demand — its SSP infrastructure gives it direct publisher relationships that a pure DSP cannot replicate. Equativ's Meta Deals and curation-first approach give buyers a different kind of inventory access than Trade Desk's open marketplace.

**What agencies use Equativ?**
All six Big Six Advertising Agencies use Equativ, along with IPG Mediabrands, Omnicom Media Group (including OMD, Kinesso), and hundreds of independent agencies across 19 countries.

**Does Equativ support retail media?**
Yes. Equativ offers retail media solutions for both retailers looking to monetize their audiences and agencies looking to access premium retail inventory and first-party purchase data at scale.

## Equativ vs Alternatives

### Equativ vs The Trade Desk
[The Trade Desk](/tools/the-trade-desk) is a pure-play DSP with Kokai AI for open marketplace buying. Equativ provides both supply and demand infrastructure, with its SSP giving curated direct access to publisher inventory. Equativ's Meta Deals and AI planning agent are differentiated for agencies that want structured PMP buying, while Trade Desk suits brands that prioritize open programmatic scale.

### Equativ vs Google DV360
[Google DV360](/tools/google-dv360) offers unmatched access to Google and YouTube inventory. Equativ cannot match that walled-garden access but provides greater independence and transparency for the open web, CTV, and premium publisher buying. For brands that want to avoid Google dependency, Equativ is a strong alternative.

### Equativ vs Amazon DSP
[Amazon DSP](/tools/amazon-dsp) is purpose-built around Amazon's shopper data. Equativ competes for premium open-web and CTV inventory without requiring Amazon's retail context.

### Equativ vs Basis
[Basis](/tools/basis) is an integrated workflow platform for agencies covering DSP, search, social, and planning in one interface. Equativ focuses more specifically on programmatic curation, supply quality, and deal management — a more specialized tool for programmatic teams.
