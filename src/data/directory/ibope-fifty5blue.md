**Latin America's primary TV audience measurement currency and cross-media analytics platform**

Ibope (formerly Kantar IBOPE Media) is the dominant television audience measurement service in Latin America — the equivalent of Nielsen ratings in the US market. Providing the official TV ratings currency that broadcasters, agencies, and advertisers use to buy and sell television advertising across Brazil and wider Latin American markets, Ibope delivers TV audience measurement, cross-media analytics, advertising insights, and media planning data. Now part of Fifty5Blue following the $1 billion spin-off from Kantar Group (H.I.G. Capital acquisition, August 2025), Ibope continues to evolve its measurement capabilities for the streaming and cross-platform era.

## Key Features

### 📺 **TV Audience Measurement**
- Official TV ratings currency across Latin American markets
- People Meter technology measuring who's watching, on which TV set, and with whom
- Processing data from over 158,000 people meters daily across Fifty5Blue's global network
- Real-time ratings data enabling same-day programming and scheduling decisions

### 📊 **Cross-Media Analytics**
- Cross-Media Planner that fuses panel data with YouTube data for next-generation planning
- Unified view of broadcast and streaming audiences
- Content and advertising measurement across linear TV and digital video
- Bridges traditional TV measurement with digital viewing behavior

### 🎯 **Media Planning Data**
- Data and analysis from planning through campaign measurement
- Audience profiling for media selection and channel optimization
- Serves as the foundational data layer for media buying negotiations
- Supports broadcasters, agencies, and advertisers with consistent metrics

### ⚡ **Real-Time & In-Flight Capabilities**
- Real-time audience data for programming decisions
- In-flight campaign monitoring and adjustment
- Near-instant feedback on audience response to content and advertising
- Globosat doubled audiences during the World Cup using real-time Ibope data for programming changes

### 📡 **Advanced Measurement Technology**
- Proprietary content and advertising recognition technology
- Focal Meter (router meter) for measuring all viewing on every device on the home network
- Measures viewing activity across TVs, tablets, PCs, and smartphones
- In-home and out-of-home measurement capabilities

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Ibope?**
Ibope (now part of Fifty5Blue) is the primary TV audience measurement service in Latin America. It provides the official TV ratings that broadcasters, agencies, and advertisers use to buy and sell television advertising.

**Is Ibope the same as Nielsen?**
Ibope serves a similar function to Nielsen in the US — it's the TV ratings currency. In Latin American markets, Ibope is the dominant player, while Nielsen leads in the US.

**How much does Ibope data cost?**
Ibope pricing is enterprise-only and not publicly available. Costs depend on market, data products, delivery method, and user type.

**Who owns Ibope now?**
Ibope is part of Fifty5Blue, which was spun off from Kantar Group and acquired by H.I.G. Capital for $1 billion in August 2025. Previously known as Kantar IBOPE Media.

**Does Ibope measure streaming?**
Yes. Ibope's Cross-Media Planner in Brazil fuses panel data with YouTube data, and Fifty5Blue is investing in hybrid measurement that covers streaming, social, and broadcast.

## Ibope vs Alternatives

### Ibope vs Nielsen
Nielsen is the TV currency in the US and several other markets. Ibope is the currency in Latin America. They don't directly compete in most markets — agencies use whichever is the local standard. Both are investing in cross-platform measurement.

### Ibope vs Comscore
[Comscore](/comscore) provides cross-platform measurement with a stronger digital focus. Ibope is the official TV ratings currency in LATAM. In many Latin American markets, agencies use Ibope for TV and Comscore for digital measurement.

### Ibope vs TechEdge
[TechEdge](/techedge) is Fifty5Blue's analytics platform — it's the software layer that agencies use to analyze and plan with Ibope data. They work together: Ibope provides the data, TechEdge provides the analysis tools.
