**The foundational platform for omnichannel advertising infrastructure**

Mediaocean powers the advertising ecosystem with AI automation and omnichannel orchestration across channels, formats, and devices. As a foundational platform used by holding companies, agencies, and enterprise brands, Mediaocean consolidates ad infrastructure and ad tech to give teams full control over global media and creative operations. Its Prisma platform handles media buying and campaign management, while Innovid (acquired by Mediaocean) powers ad serving and personalization.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does Mediaocean cost?**
Mediaocean does not publish pricing. It uses an enterprise, contact-sales model with custom quotes based on organization size, volume, and product scope. The platform is positioned for large agencies, holding companies, and enterprise brands — not small businesses or startups.

**Is Mediaocean free?**
No. Mediaocean is a premium enterprise platform. There is no free tier, trial, or self-serve option. Access requires a sales engagement and contract.

**What is Prisma in Mediaocean?**
Prisma is Mediaocean's core media buying and campaign management platform. It handles planning, buying, billing, and reconciliation for omnichannel campaigns. Users frequently cite Prisma's ability to flag errors and manage invoices as key workflow advantages.

**How does Mediaocean compare to Basis?**
[Basis](/basis) is a digital advertising automation platform focused on agencies running programmatic, search, social, and CTV campaigns. Mediaocean is a broader infrastructure platform used by holding companies and enterprise brands to manage all media — including traditional. Basis has stronger DSP capabilities; Mediaocean has deeper financial workflow and global scale.

**How does Mediaocean compare to Adobe Advertising Cloud?**
[Adobe Advertising Cloud](/adobe-advertising-cloud) offers programmatic buying and cross-channel campaign management within Adobe's marketing stack. Mediaocean is a broader operational infrastructure platform covering the full planning-to-reconciliation lifecycle. Adobe is a better fit for teams deeply embedded in the Adobe ecosystem; Mediaocean for large multi-agency or holding company operations.

**Does Mediaocean have AI features?**
Yes. Mediaocean has invested significantly in AI, including the Moe AI assistant within Prisma, and AI Agents through Innovid (launched November 2025). The company published a 2026 Advertising Outlook Report in February 2026 focused on AI's rise in media operations.

**What size organization is Mediaocean best suited for?**
Mediaocean is designed for large agencies, holding companies, and enterprise brands. Smaller agencies or independent shops often find lighter tools like [Basis](/basis), [Bionic](/bionic), or [Camphouse](/camphouse) more practical and cost-effective.

## Mediaocean vs Alternatives

### Mediaocean vs Basis
[Basis](/basis) by Basis Technologies is an enterprise advertising automation platform emphasizing programmatic DSP capabilities, omnichannel planning, and workflow automation. Basis is often seen as more agency-focused and easier to implement than Mediaocean. The two companies announced a foundational partnership in January 2026 to automate the end-to-end media lifecycle — suggesting many organizations use both.

### Mediaocean vs Adobe Advertising Cloud
[Adobe Advertising Cloud](/adobe-advertising-cloud) provides programmatic buying, search, and social management as part of Adobe's broader enterprise marketing suite. It's a strong fit for organizations already using Adobe Experience Cloud. Mediaocean has a larger footprint in traditional media planning and holding company workflows.

### Mediaocean vs The Trade Desk
[The Trade Desk](/the-trade-desk) is a demand-side platform (DSP) focused on programmatic buying and data-driven targeting. Mediaocean is an infrastructure platform — the two are often used together, with Mediaocean handling planning/workflow and The Trade Desk handling programmatic execution.

### Mediaocean vs Amazon DSP
[Amazon DSP](/amazon-dsp) focuses on programmatic access to Amazon's inventory and first-party shopping data. Mediaocean is an omnichannel infrastructure platform that can connect to Amazon DSP and other programmatic channels as part of a unified workflow.

### Mediaocean vs Bionic
[Bionic](/bionic) is a media planning and flowchart tool designed for agency planners. It's significantly more accessible and affordable than Mediaocean. Bionic is better suited for independent agencies focused on planning and reporting; Mediaocean covers the full operational lifecycle at enterprise scale.
