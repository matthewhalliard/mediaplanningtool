**The audience analytics and media planning toolkit built for the hybrid measurement era**

TechEdge is Fifty5Blue's (formerly Kantar Media) flagship software platform for accessing, analyzing, and planning with audience data. Used in 70+ markets globally, it connects panel data, big data, and digital signals into a single view — letting agencies, broadcasters, and advertisers plan, analyze, and report on audiences across channels and platforms. Following Fifty5Blue's $1 billion spin-off from Kantar Group (acquired by H.I.G. Capital in August 2025), TechEdge remains central to the portfolio and is receiving accelerated investment in AI and cross-media capabilities.

## Key Features

### 📊 **Audience Analysis & Planning**
- View, compare, and plan using local and global audience data by channel, platform, and programme
- Deep-dive into audience data to create optimized media plans
- Build dynamic audience target groups based on viewing behavior
- Unified data view for global teams — consistent planning and research across countries

### ⚡ **AdvantEdge — Reach & Frequency Engine**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is TechEdge?**
TechEdge is Fifty5Blue's (formerly Kantar Media) audience analytics and media planning platform. It connects audience data from panels, big data, and digital sources into one view for planning, analysis, and campaign evaluation.

**How much does TechEdge cost?**
TechEdge pricing is enterprise-only and not publicly listed. Costs depend on markets covered, product modules licensed (AdvantEdge, K2, KxM, AudEx), and data delivery method.

**What is AdvantEdge?**
AdvantEdge is TechEdge's high-performance reach & frequency analysis engine. It processes panel data in under 30 seconds and big data in under 75 seconds.

**How does TechEdge handle cross-media measurement?**
TechEdge combines panel-based data with large-scale digital data (set-top box, smart TV ACR, streaming) to provide a deduplicated, people-based view of audiences across TV, streaming, social, and digital.

TechEdge is part of Fifty5Blue, which was spun off from Kantar Group and acquired by H.I.G. Capital for $1 billion in August 2025. The company rebranded from Kantar Media to Fifty5Blue in February 2026.

## TechEdge vs Alternatives

### TechEdge vs Nielsen Media Impact
[Nielsen Media Impact](/nielsen-media-impact) is a planning and simulation tool focused on the US market. TechEdge operates in 70+ markets and combines panel + big data in a hybrid model. Nielsen is stronger for US TV currency; TechEdge offers broader global coverage and faster R&F processing.

### TechEdge vs Comscore
[Comscore](/comscore) focuses on cross-platform measurement and Proximic activation. TechEdge is more of a planning and analytics workbench — agencies use TechEdge to build plans and evaluate campaigns, while Comscore provides measurement currency and targeting segments.

### TechEdge vs GWI
[GWI](/gwi) provides survey-based global audience insights for profiling and targeting. TechEdge is an analytics platform that ingests measurement data (panels + big data) for planning and campaign evaluation. GWI feeds the "who" insight; TechEdge powers the "how much" planning.

### TechEdge vs MRI-Simmons
[MRI-Simmons](/mri-simmons) is consumer research and audience profiling (attitudes, media consumption). TechEdge is the analytics engine for media planning and campaign measurement. Many agencies use MRI-Simmons for audience definition and TechEdge for planning execution.
