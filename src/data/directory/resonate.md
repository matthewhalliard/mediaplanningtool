## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does Resonate cost?**
Resonate does not publish pricing. It is an enterprise platform with custom pricing based on use case and data requirements. Contact Resonate's sales team for a quote.

**Is Resonate free?**
No. Resonate is an enterprise product with no free tier or trial. It is designed for brands and agencies with significant audience data and targeting needs.

**What is the Ignite platform?**
Ignite is Resonate's core product — a unified consumer intelligence platform for building audiences, generating insights, and activating data across channels. It houses Resonate's 250M consumer profiles and the rAI engine that powers predictive attributes.

**How does Resonate compare to MRI-Simmons?**
[MRI-Simmons](/mri-simmons) is a survey-based consumer research database used for media planning and audience profiling, particularly for traditional media. Resonate covers a broader set of 250M digital profiles with real-time predictive attributes. MRI-Simmons is stronger for traditional media audience research; Resonate is better for digital targeting and first-party data enrichment.

**How does Resonate compare to Comscore?**
[Comscore](/comscore) focuses on media measurement — audience reach, frequency, and cross-platform exposure. Resonate is a consumer intelligence platform focused on the who behind audiences — their values, motivations, and behaviors. They serve different functions in a media planning stack.

**What makes Resonate different from a DMP or CDP?**
Resonate goes beyond identity matching. Its rAI engine predicts 15K+ consumer attributes — including psychographics and motivations — not just behavioral signals. This makes it a consumer intelligence layer that can enrich CDPs and DMPs with deeper profile data.

**Does Resonate have AI capabilities?**
Yes. The rAI engine powers Resonate's predictive attributes across 250M profiles. The platform also includes a newer AI feature for automated data aggregation, allowing users to get AI-generated summaries of audience insights without manual analysis.

## Resonate vs Alternatives

### Resonate vs MRI-Simmons
[MRI-Simmons](/mri-simmons) is the standard consumer research database for traditional media audience planning in the US. It's survey-based with deep demographic and media behavior data. Resonate provides a larger digital profile base (250M vs. survey panel) with real-time predictive attributes. MRI-Simmons is often preferred for upfront media planning; Resonate for digital activation and CRM enrichment.

### Resonate vs Comscore
[Comscore](/comscore) specializes in media measurement — measuring who is exposed to media, across TV, digital, and audio. Resonate focuses on consumer intelligence — understanding the motivations and behaviors that drive consumer decisions. Most large planning teams use both for different purposes.

### Resonate vs GWI
GWI (formerly GlobalWebIndex) is a survey-based consumer intelligence platform covering global markets. Resonate is US-focused with a larger profile base and real-time predictive modeling. GWI has a broader geographic footprint; Resonate offers deeper attribute depth for US audiences.

### Resonate vs Commspoint Influence
[Commspoint Influence](/commspoint-influence) uses Nielsen audience data for channel mix strategy. Resonate provides consumer intelligence at the individual profile level, enabling granular audience segmentation and activation. Resonate is better suited for teams needing behavioral and psychographic depth; Commspoint Influence for strategic channel planning.
