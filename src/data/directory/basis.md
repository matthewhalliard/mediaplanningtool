## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does Basis cost?**
Basis does not publish pricing. It is an enterprise platform with custom pricing based on agency size, volume, and channel mix. No public tiers are listed. Contact Basis directly for a quote.

**Is Basis free?**
No. Basis is a paid enterprise platform with no free tier or self-serve option. A dedicated sales process is required to access the platform.

**What happened to Centro — is Basis the same company?**
Yes. Basis Technologies was formerly known as Centro. The company rebranded to Basis Technologies in 2022, with the platform retaining the Basis name. The rebrand reflected the company's evolution beyond its media buying origins into a full advertising automation platform.

**How does Basis compare to Mediaocean?**
[Mediaocean](/mediaocean) is a broader omnichannel infrastructure platform used primarily by holding companies and large enterprise brands, with strong traditional media workflow capabilities. Basis is more agency-focused, with a stronger programmatic DSP component. The two companies announced a foundational partnership in January 2026 to automate the media lifecycle end-to-end, and many organizations use both.

**How does Basis compare to The Trade Desk?**
[The Trade Desk](/the-trade-desk) is a pure programmatic DSP with industry-leading data and audience capabilities. Basis includes its own DSP but also covers planning, search, social, and reconciliation workflows. Agencies looking for the strongest pure-play programmatic capabilities often choose The Trade Desk; those needing a unified planning-to-billing platform lean toward Basis.

**Does Basis have AI capabilities?**
Yes. Basis has invested in AI across the platform, including Basis Assistant for automated campaign insights and advanced machine learning for audience identification and optimization. AI capabilities are central to its 2025–2026 product strategy.

**What industries use Basis most?**
Basis is used primarily by media agencies, digital marketing agencies, and enterprise brand teams managing complex omnichannel digital campaigns. It's particularly strong for agencies running programmatic alongside search and social from a single workflow.

## Basis vs Alternatives

### Basis vs Mediaocean
[Mediaocean](/mediaocean) is an enterprise omnichannel infrastructure platform used by holding companies and large brands. Basis is more commonly used by mid-to-large agencies focused on digital campaign execution. The two platforms announced a foundational integration partnership in January 2026, positioning them as complementary rather than purely competitive.

### Basis vs The Trade Desk
[The Trade Desk](/the-trade-desk) is the dominant independent programmatic DSP, with deep data integrations and advanced audience tools. Basis offers a DSP component but differentiates on workflow automation, planning, and reconciliation. Teams seeking pure programmatic power typically prefer The Trade Desk; teams needing full advertising operations automation lean toward Basis.

### Basis vs Google DV360
[Google DV360](/google-dv360) is Google's enterprise demand-side platform with deep integration into Google's ad ecosystem. It's a strong choice for teams heavy on YouTube and Google inventory. Basis provides a platform-agnostic alternative that includes non-Google programmatic inventory alongside search and social channel management.

### Basis vs Bionic
[Bionic](/bionic) is a media planning and flowchart tool for agency planners. It does not include programmatic buying or campaign execution. Bionic is a planning front-end; Basis is a full planning-to-execution platform. Some agencies use both.
