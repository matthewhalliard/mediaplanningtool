**Halliard drafts a media plan from a brief, models its reach and frequency, and can buy the plan if you want it to.**

Halliard publishes this directory, so this profile describes our own product. Everything below comes from Halliard's own site, checked on November 16, 2026.

## What it does

- **Drafts the plan.** You paste a brief or drop in an RFP. Halliard says it drafts the audiences, strategy and channel plan in about six minutes.
- **Models reach and frequency.** Reach and frequency are modelled for every strategy in the plan, down to the channel and the property.
- **Lets you edit any line.** Each line shows budget, impressions, CPM, reach and fit. Change one and the plan rebalances.
- **Sizes audiences locally.** Catchments inside a market, ZIP codes ranked against the brief, and search sized by market.
- **Covers the main channels.** TV, streaming, social and outdoor can sit in one plan.
- **Works for one brand or many.** Agencies switch between clients in one login.

## Buying is optional

The plan is yours either way. You can buy it the way you do today, or hand the approved plan to Halliard.

If Halliard buys it:

- Campaigns go live on PubMatic and Meta.
- A person approves every line before money is spent.
- Delivery is checked every six hours. Bigger changes wait for approval; small budget adjustments run on their own and are logged.
- Every action is recorded: what was bought, why, and what the platform said back.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Halliard free?**
Planning is free for a team planning on its own, with no credit card. An agency putting a whole planning team on it pays $13,000 a year. Buying and measurement are paid per campaign or project.

**How much does Halliard cost to buy media?**
A fee per campaign. Halliard estimates the hours a freelance media buyer would spend on the campaign and charges 30% of that cost. The fee is shown before you approve.

**What channels does Halliard plan?**
Its site names TV, streaming, social and outdoor. It buys on Meta and PubMatic.

**Do I have to buy through Halliard?**
No. You can take the plan and buy it yourself.
