**Free programmatic media planner powered by live DSP benchmarks and Bellwether-verified inventory quality.**

AdLib Planner is a media planning tool built for advertising agencies to create data-backed programmatic media plans in minutes. Rather than relying on outdated industry averages, it pulls live CPM data from real AdLib DSP campaigns to power reach, frequency, and conversion forecasts. The platform includes a Site List Generator that produces campaign-ready, ads.txt-verified site lists by vertical and audience — filtering out MFA (made-for-advertising) inventory. Built by former MediaMath veterans Mike Hauptman and Dan Bougourd, AdLib Planner is the planning interface for the broader AdLib Media Group ecosystem, which includes a Meta DSP with access to 20+ enterprise demand-side platforms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does AdLib Planner cost?**
AdLib Planner is free forever for individual planners. No credit card is required to sign up. Team plans for agencies needing collaboration features are available at undisclosed pricing.

**What is the Site List Generator?**
The Site List Generator lets you input a vertical and audience to receive a curated, campaign-ready site list. Every domain is ads.txt verified, and the lists exclude MFA (made-for-advertising) sites. Pre-built lists cover 23+ verticals including automotive, beauty, business, entertainment, finance, health, news, sports, tech, and travel.

**How does AdLib Planner compare to Bionic?**
[Bionic](/bionic) is a full-suite media planning and buying platform priced at $199/user/month with a 5-user minimum. AdLib Planner is a free, programmatic-focused planning tool with live CPM benchmarks from its own DSP. Bionic covers all media channels (TV, radio, print, digital); AdLib Planner specializes in programmatic. Agencies needing omnichannel planning may prefer Bionic; those focused on winning programmatic RFPs with real benchmark data may prefer AdLib Planner.

**How does AdLib Planner compare to Halliard?**
[Halliard](/halliard) is a connected media planning platform with cross-channel reach and frequency forecasting across 18+ channels and agentic media buying via PubMatic MCP. AdLib Planner focuses specifically on programmatic planning with live DSP benchmarks. Halliard offers broader planning-to-execution workflows; AdLib Planner offers deeper programmatic CPM intelligence and free entry-level access.

**How does AdLib Planner compare to Mediatool?**
[Mediatool](/mediatool) is a cloud-based media management platform for tracking spend and performance across channels. AdLib Planner is a planning-first tool focused on generating programmatic media plans with live CPM data. Mediatool is stronger on ongoing campaign management; AdLib Planner is stronger on upfront plan creation and RFP response.

**Is AdLib Planner only for programmatic media?**
AdLib Planner is focused on programmatic media planning. For agencies needing cross-channel planning that includes traditional media (TV, radio, print, OOH), tools like [Halliard](/halliard), [Bionic](/bionic), or [Strata](/strata) may be more appropriate.

**Who founded AdLib?**
AdLib Media Group was founded by Mike Hauptman (CEO) and Dan Bougourd (CTO), both long-time MediaMath employees. Mike was among the first 100 employees at MediaMath; Dan served as VP of Engineering there for 11 years. The company is bootstrapped and profitable with no VC funding.

**Where are AdLib Planner's CPM benchmarks sourced from?**
The benchmarks come from real, live campaigns running through the AdLib DSP — not from surveys, panels, or industry reports. This gives agencies access to current market pricing rather than outdated averages.
