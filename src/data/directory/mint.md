**AI-powered media planning and buying platform for enterprise teams managing complex, multi-channel campaigns**

MINT.ai (formerly Myntelligence Mint) is an enterprise media planning platform that uses a Multi-Agent AI system to automate and optimize media workflows. Designed for large advertising teams and agencies, it consolidates cross-channel planning, campaign actualization, and predictive insights into a single platform — reducing manual effort and improving campaign performance.

## Key Features

### 🤖 **Multi-Agent AI System**
- Launched March 2025: multiple AI agents collaborate across media planning tasks
- Automated actualization of media buys against planned targets
- Predictive planning with AI-generated insights and recommendations
- Smart budget reallocation based on performance signals
- AI-driven anomaly detection across active campaigns

### 📋 **Cross-Channel Planning Hub**
- Unified planning environment across digital, broadcast, print, and OOH
- Centralized view of all campaigns and media investments
- Consistent workflow from plan creation through post-campaign analysis
- Scenario modeling for budget and channel mix optimization

### ⚡ **Automated Workflows & Actualization**
- Automates the labor-intensive process of reconciling plan vs. actual delivery
- Streamlined approval and sign-off workflows
- Reduces manual data entry and discrepancy resolution time
- Campaign status tracking with real-time alerts

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does MINT.ai cost?**
MINT.ai uses custom enterprise pricing based on annual media spend, number of seats, and number of campaigns managed. There is no publicly listed price — contact MINT.ai for a demo and pricing proposal.

**Is MINT.ai the same as Mint the personal finance app?**
No. MINT.ai (formerly Myntelligence Mint) is an enterprise media planning and buying platform, entirely separate from the consumer budgeting app Mint that was discontinued by Intuit. MINT.ai is specifically built for advertising and media teams.

**What is the Multi-Agent AI system in MINT.ai?**
Launched in March 2025, MINT.ai's Multi-Agent System deploys multiple AI agents that work collaboratively on media planning tasks — from automating actualization to generating predictive planning insights. Each agent handles a specialized function, reducing the need for manual intervention across the campaign lifecycle.

**How does MINT.ai compare to Mediaocean?**
Both MINT.ai and [Mediaocean](/mediaocean) serve enterprise media operations. Mediaocean has deeper roots in financial management and broadcast buying, while MINT.ai differentiates with its AI-first approach to planning automation and its Multi-Agent AI system for real-time campaign management.

**How does MINT.ai compare to Basis?**
[Basis](/basis) is a strong digital-first media management platform with integrated DSP capabilities. MINT.ai covers a broader channel mix including traditional media, and its AI automation layer is more developed for complex actualization workflows at enterprise scale.

**Does MINT.ai offer a free trial?**
No free trial is publicly offered. MINT.ai is an enterprise product with a consultative sales process. Demos are available upon request.

## MINT.ai vs Alternatives

### MINT.ai vs Mediaocean
[Mediaocean](/mediaocean) is the established enterprise media management standard, known for financial reconciliation and broadcast buying. MINT.ai competes by offering a more modern AI-driven planning layer and automated actualization — appealing to teams looking to reduce manual reconciliation work.

### MINT.ai vs Basis
[Basis](/basis) integrates planning, buying, and DSP capabilities for digital-first teams. MINT.ai covers a wider channel scope and focuses more on enterprise workflow automation across both digital and traditional channels.

### MINT.ai vs Halliard
[Halliard](/halliard) is a more accessible media planning OS with a free individual plan. MINT.ai targets enterprise-scale operations with its AI agents and deep actualization capabilities. Teams at the enterprise level with high actualization complexity may evaluate MINT.ai; smaller and mid-market teams often find Halliard a better fit.

### MINT.ai vs Camphouse
[Camphouse](/camphouse) offers a collaborative planning interface for global teams. MINT.ai differentiates with its AI automation layer, particularly for actualization and predictive planning at larger campaign volumes.
