**LinkedIn's B2B advertising platform for precision professional audience targeting**

LinkedIn Campaign Manager is the primary advertising interface for LinkedIn's network of over 1 billion professionals across 200+ countries. It enables B2B advertisers to reach decision-makers by job title, function, seniority, company, industry, and professional skills — targeting attributes unavailable on any other platform at this scale. In 2025 and 2026, LinkedIn significantly expanded its AI capabilities, including the Accelerate automated campaign tool, AI-generated ad creatives, and predictive audience modeling.

## Pricing

LinkedIn Campaign Manager uses **auction-based pricing** — there are no fixed subscription tiers or platform fees.

- **Minimum daily budget**: approximately $10 per campaign
- **CPC (cost per click)**: typically $2–$9, varies by audience and competition
- **CPM (cost per thousand impressions)**: typically $6–$20
- **CPS (cost per send)**: applies to Sponsored Messaging campaigns
- **No platform fee**: you pay only for ad performance and engagement

LinkedIn's CPCs and CPMs are higher than most social platforms, reflecting the professional audience premium. Budget management and monitoring are essential — reviewers consistently note that unmonitored campaigns can deplete budgets quickly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is LinkedIn Campaign Manager free to use?**
The Campaign Manager interface is free to access. You pay only when your campaigns run — via auction-based CPC, CPM, or CPS pricing. There are no platform subscription fees.

**What is the minimum budget for LinkedIn ads?**
LinkedIn requires a minimum daily budget of approximately $10 per campaign. Practical minimum spending for meaningful reach in competitive B2B segments tends to be considerably higher.

**Why are LinkedIn ads so expensive compared to other platforms?**
LinkedIn's CPC and CPM rates reflect the premium value of its professional audience data. Targeting a CFO in a specific industry at a specific company size is not possible at LinkedIn's scale on other platforms. Advertisers pay more per click, but often see higher lead quality and conversion rates in B2B contexts.

**How does LinkedIn Campaign Manager compare to Meta for B2B advertising?**
LinkedIn offers unmatched professional targeting depth — job title, seniority, company size — that Meta cannot replicate. Meta typically delivers lower CPCs and broader reach, but with less precise professional targeting. B2B campaigns with niche professional audiences (e.g., enterprise IT buyers) generally perform better on LinkedIn; broader awareness campaigns with larger budgets often use both.

**What is LinkedIn Accelerate?**
Accelerate is LinkedIn's AI-powered automated campaign tool, launched in 2025. It uses your objective and inputs to automatically recommend audience targeting, budget, ad format, and creative variations. It is designed for advertisers who want automated optimization rather than manual campaign management.

**Does LinkedIn Campaign Manager support account-based marketing?**
Yes. LinkedIn's Matched Audiences feature allows targeting by uploaded company lists, contact lists, and website visitor retargeting. Combined with Sales Navigator integration, it is one of the strongest ABM advertising platforms available.

## LinkedIn Campaign Manager vs Alternatives

### LinkedIn Campaign Manager vs Meta Business Manager
[Meta Business Manager](/meta-business-manager) reaches a far larger total audience and delivers lower CPCs across the board. For B2C campaigns or broad awareness, Meta often outperforms LinkedIn on cost efficiency. For B2B campaigns requiring professional attribute targeting — job title, company, industry — LinkedIn has no comparable rival. Most B2B teams run both platforms with different objectives.

### LinkedIn Campaign Manager vs Google Ads
Google Ads captures demand at the search intent level, while LinkedIn creates demand within a professional context. Google is typically better for bottom-funnel campaigns where prospects are actively searching; LinkedIn is stronger for top- and mid-funnel B2B campaigns targeting specific professional audiences.

### LinkedIn Campaign Manager vs TikTok Ads Manager
[TikTok Ads Manager](/tiktok-ads-manager) is increasingly used for B2B brand awareness among younger professional audiences. TikTok offers significantly lower CPMs than LinkedIn but far less professional targeting precision. Some B2B brands use TikTok for awareness alongside LinkedIn for conversion-focused campaigns.

### LinkedIn Campaign Manager vs The Trade Desk
[The Trade Desk](/the-trade-desk) provides programmatic access to display, video, and CTV inventory with advanced audience data. For B2B advertisers seeking professional audience targeting at scale beyond LinkedIn's own inventory, The Trade Desk with business audience data segments can complement LinkedIn-focused campaigns.
