**Unified TV+ advertising platform for data-driven linear TV and CTV planning, activation, and measurement**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does Simulmedia cost?**
Simulmedia's managed campaign service has enterprise pricing with minimum spend requirements — exact amounts are not publicly listed. The Skybeam self-serve platform offers free planning insights, with campaign costs based on actual media spend. Contact Simulmedia for managed campaign pricing.

**What is the minimum spend for a Simulmedia managed campaign?**
Simulmedia does not publish a specific minimum publicly, but user reviews note that the minimum can be a barrier for smaller advertisers. The self-serve Skybeam platform is the recommended starting point for brands with smaller budgets.

**What is Skybeam?**
Skybeam is Simulmedia's self-serve TV advertising platform. It was updated in November 2025 to include traditional (linear) TV advertising alongside CTV, giving advertisers direct access to TV inventory with lower barriers than the full managed service.

**How does Simulmedia compare to The Trade Desk for CTV?**
[The Trade Desk](/the-trade-desk) is a broad programmatic DSP covering CTV alongside display, audio, and digital OOH. Simulmedia is more specialized — its strength is specifically in unified linear TV and CTV, with 250+ direct network integrations. Advertisers who want TV-first planning and optimization often find Simulmedia more purpose-built for that use case.

**Does Simulmedia support connected TV (CTV) and linear TV together?**
Yes. The TV+ platform is explicitly designed to unify linear and CTV planning, activation, and measurement — enabling true cross-TV reach and frequency analysis rather than treating each channel separately.

**What verticals does Simulmedia serve?**
Simulmedia serves a broad range of national advertisers. The June 2025 partnership with OptimizeRx specifically extends targeting capabilities for healthcare and pharmaceutical advertisers.

## Simulmedia vs Alternatives

### Simulmedia vs The Trade Desk
[The Trade Desk](/the-trade-desk) is the dominant independent DSP for programmatic advertising, including CTV. Simulmedia is more specialized in unified linear + CTV — its 250+ direct network integrations and TV-first optimization engine make it a stronger choice for advertisers whose primary focus is television rather than broad programmatic.

### Simulmedia vs Amazon DSP
[Amazon DSP](/amazon-dsp) offers CTV reach through Amazon's streaming inventory (Prime Video, Fire TV) and third-party channels. Simulmedia provides broader access across 250+ networks including both streaming and traditional broadcast, without relying on Amazon's ecosystem.

### Simulmedia vs Google DV360
[Google DV360](/google-dv360) is a comprehensive DSP with CTV capabilities. For advertisers primarily focused on TV, Simulmedia's purpose-built TV+ platform and linear TV integration go deeper than DV360's broader programmatic toolkit.

### Simulmedia vs Halliard
[Halliard](/halliard) is a media planning OS for teams managing campaigns across all channels. Simulmedia is a specialized activation platform for TV. Teams often use Halliard for planning and cross-channel visibility while activating specific TV buys through platforms like Simulmedia.
